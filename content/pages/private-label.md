## [why] Why a hammam line is a smart first range

- **It is a ritual, so it sells as a set.** A kit of three products sells for more than the three sold separately.
- **The story is built in.** You do not need to invent a concept; the Moroccan hammam already has one.
- **Few products, wide appeal.** Three or four items cover the whole routine, so your launch stock stays small.
- **Repeat purchases.** Black soap and clay run out; customers come back.

## [how-it-works] How it works

1. **Brief (day 1):** tell us your market, products, quantities and budget.
2. **Samples (week 1 to 2):** we send samples of the products and packaging you shortlist.
3. **Design (week 2 to 3):** you send your logo, or our designer prepares labels from your brief. You approve a final proof.
4. **Production (week 3 to 6):** we produce, fill and label. You get photos of the finished batch.
5. **Documents and shipping:** COA, MSDS, INCI list, certificate of origin. Shipped by courier, air or sea.

## [labels] Labels and compliance

Your label must meet the rules of the country you sell in. For the EU, that means an EU Responsible Person, INCI list, batch number, net weight and period-after-opening symbol. For the US, FDA cosmetic labeling rules apply. We supply the INCI list and product data from Agadir; you or your Responsible Person confirm final compliance.

## [mistakes] Mistakes to avoid

- **Ordering too many variants at launch.** Start with 3 to 4 products and add later.
- **Choosing packaging before testing the product in it.** Black soap is a paste; some pumps and tubes do not work with it.
- **Forgetting shipping weight.** Glass jars and clay are heavy; compare air vs sea freight before choosing packaging.
- **Skipping label approval.** Always sign a printed proof before production.
